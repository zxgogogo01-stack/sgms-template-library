About the journal

[[SITE_NAME]] publishes literary essays about attention, place, work, memory, and the textures of ordinary time. We prefer patient observation to hot takes, precise language to performance, and a publishing rhythm that leaves room for revision.

The journal is edited from [[LOCATION]]. Unless a piece says otherwise, its words and illustrations are original to this publication.

Editorial independence

Editorial choices are made for readers, not advertisers. Sponsorship, affiliate relationships, or gifted materials—if we ever accept them—will be disclosed beside the relevant work. A commercial relationship will not determine the conclusion of an essay or review.

Accuracy & corrections

Literary essays can be subjective; factual claims still deserve care. We check names, dates, quotations, and material context before publication. If we make a substantive error, we correct it in place and add a dated note describing what changed.

To request a correction

Write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] with the article title, the passage in question, and a source we can review. We acknowledge good-faith requests and aim to assess them promptly.

Letters & contributions

Thoughtful reader letters are welcome. Please keep them under 500 words and tell us whether they may be edited for length and clarity. Unsolicited essay submissions are reviewed only when the journal announces an open reading period.

We do not publish harassment, private personal information, or material that misrepresents another person’s work as the writer’s own.

Sentence tool privacy

The sentence and rhythm analyzer runs entirely in your browser. Text entered there is not transmitted to us, added to analytics, or stored by this template. Closing or refreshing the page clears the working text unless your browser preserves form state locally.

We recommend removing confidential, identifying, or legally sensitive information before placing it into any writing utility.
